Cauldron Inn is a story-driven fairy-tale psychological horror game centered around anomaly hunting.

You wake up trapped inside a mysterious hotel from the early 20th century. To escape, you must carefully examine your surroundings and find the differences — anomalies that distort reality.

The early 20th century. A Siberian forest. Something sinister is pursuing you when suddenly you spot a building—an inn—and take refuge inside. But this temporary sanctuary becomes an eternal prison; time stands still, and the memory of your life vanishes in an instant.

However, you find instructions that will allow you to escape. You need to keep an eye on what is happening in the hotel. Anomalies may appear there from time to time—paranormal manifestations of evil forces. Sometimes they are hard to spot, but at other times, they actively pursue you. Whenever you discover an anomaly, you must ring the bell in one of the rooms.

What was chasing you? Someone is sending you messages in the form of straw dolls—who is it? Play the game to the end to uncover a deep narrative steeped in legend and dread, where the fates of several characters intertwine.

The player’s main goal is to search for anomalies — strange things that randomly appear in the Inn. Finding them 8 times in a row leads to victory.

About Me

Hi! My name is Pavel. I am a technical artist, art director, solo video game developer, and a resident of a Moscow game hub.

Cauldron Inn is my first major game project, which I have been developing since late 2025. The game is rooted in the dark fairy tales that have long fascinated me and that I occasionally write myself.

I strove to create the atmosphere of a bygone era—the turn of the century—and drew upon the Slavic aesthetic of dark storytelling when crafting the game’s anomalies. Also you will encounter several memorable characters throughout the story.

I hope players will be able to see the soul I poured into this game.
